Details read from your pages, including Apple Intelligence suggestions, can be wrong. You confirm every detail and are responsible for what you save.

Reminders and gift cards

Reminders are best-effort. They depend on the dates you saved and on iOS delivering notifications, so don't rely on them alone for important deadlines.

Gift card balances are what you enter. Paperwell doesn't connect to merchants, so check your balance with the merchant.

Your keys and backups

Only you can open your vault. We can't reset or recover it. If you lose both your vault key and your recovery key, no one can recover your documents.

Keep your recovery key safe and save an encrypted backup from time to time.

Emergency codes don't protect against someone forcing you to use Face ID, or against forensic tools.

You are responsible for using emergency codes lawfully.

Plans

Paperwell Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds follow Apple's terms.

If a plan ends, you have 30 days to view, search, edit, delete, share, export and back up everything. Adding new items and Apple Intelligence questions need a plan. After the 30 days, the vault is paused: your data stays encrypted on your iPhone, but it can't be opened until you subscribe again or restore your purchase. Nothing is deleted, and Erase Vault is always available.

No warranty

As far as the law allows, Paperwell is provided as is, and we aren't liable for lost data, missed reminders or incorrect details.
