Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026 · Applies to Paperwell for iPhone

The short version

Paperwell has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. It works in Airplane Mode.

Everything you add stays on your iPhone. We never receive your documents, photos, gift cards or any other content, and we can't see them.

We collect no data about you or how you use the app.

What's stored, and how

Page images, photos and sensitive details (document numbers, notes, text read from your pages, and gift card numbers, PINs and balances) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key made on your iPhone.

Titles, document types, dates and people's names are kept unencrypted inside the app so lists and reminders work while your iPhone is locked. iOS data protection still covers them.

Text is read from your pages on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework.

Your vault key

Your vault key is saved in your Keychain. With iCloud Keychain on, Apple syncs it end-to-end encrypted to your other devices so a new iPhone can open your vault. We never receive it.

Anyone with your recovery key and a copy of your vault data can read your documents. Keep it safe.

Apple Intelligence

If Apple Intelligence is available and on, Paperwell uses Apple's on-device model to suggest details and answer questions about your documents. It runs only on your iPhone, never in the cloud. Without it, Paperwell uses the details you saved.

When anything leaves the app

Only when you choose: encrypted backups go where you save them; plain exports and shared files (PDF, images, CSV) aren't encrypted, and Paperwell warns you first; pictures you save go to Photos, which Paperwell can add to but not see.

Copied values stay on this device and are cleared after about a minute.

iPhone backups to iCloud or a computer include Paperwell's data in its encrypted form.

Notifications and purchases

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They never show a document's number or notes, and with "Hide details on Lock Screen" on they show no details at all.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

Adults, and deleting your data

Paperwell is intended for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Delete documents in the app, use Erase Vault (always available, whatever your plan), or delete the app.
